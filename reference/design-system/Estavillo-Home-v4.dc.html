Main case

Product + System Design · In progress

A decision system for metal workshop budgeting.

A metal fabrication workshop that quotes well — but slowly, and from one person's head. Estimates queue up, and hours go into requests that end up not closing. I'm designing a system that turns that tacit knowledge into explicit, versioned criteria: consistent, transferable, and able to answer early with an orientative range before hours are committed.

Developed and implemented at Guzmán Villalba — metal fabrication workshop, Montevideo.DRAFT
